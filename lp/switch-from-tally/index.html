Recommended, not required. Onebook’s native accounting module beats legacy systems on speed, automation, and mobile access. If you switch, we handle the entire migration and train your accounts team. If you prefer to stay, our advanced two-way sync keeps your accountant on Tally with zero workflow disruption.

What is Onebook built on?+

Onebook is built on ERPNext, the most widely deployed open-source ERP framework globally, used by 10,000+ companies across manufacturing, distribution, and services. We've customized it specifically for Indian SMEs — GST/TDS compliance, e-way bill generation, and the 2-way Tally sync layer — and back it with implementation, migration, and ongoing support that a raw open-source install won't give you on its own.

What happens to our historical Tally data?+

It transfers over perfectly. All your ledger masters, historical records, and opening balances migrate cleanly. Nothing needs to be re-typed, and absolutely nothing gets lost.

How long does implementation take?+

8 to 12 weeks, handled in gradual phases. We map out your timeline clearly so you know your exact go-live date before you even sign a contract.

Can we start with just inventory and add production later?+

Yes, absolutely. We strictly avoid chaotic, single-day switchovers. You can deploy modules in manageable phases—starting with core books, then layering on inventory, and introducing production tools whenever you are ready.

What does it cost?+

One flat price based on your business size, with unlimited users. You pay a transparent rate with no hidden fees: Micro tier at ₹4,000/month, Growth tier at ₹12,000/month, and custom pricing for large Enterprises.
