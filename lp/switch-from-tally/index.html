Questions, answered

Anything else? Ask us directly.

Can Onebook handle multi-level BOMs and process manufacturing?

Yes. ERPNext supports multi-level BOMs, routings, workstations, work orders, scrap, and by-products.

Do you support batch, expiry, and serial tracking?

Yes, from purchase receipt through production to dispatch — supporting recalls and audits.

Can we manage job work with outside vendors?

Yes. Subcontracting tracks material sent, returned, and consumed against each order.

Can we apply different prices per customer or region?

Yes. Multiple price lists, pricing rules, discounts, and schemes by customer, group, territory, or quantity.

Can we manage dealer schemes and discounts?

Yes, configured by dealer, territory, product, or quantity and applied automatically on orders.

What is Onebook built on?

Onebook is built on ERPNext, the most widely deployed open-source ERP framework globally, used by 10,000+ companies across manufacturing, distribution, and services. We've customized it for Indian SMEs — GST/TDS, e-invoicing, e-way bills, and the 2-way Tally sync layer — and back it with implementation, migration, and support.

Is my data secure on an open-source platform?

Yes. Open source means the codebase is auditable, not that data is public. Your instance runs on a private Azure/AWS server, with encrypted backups, role-based access, and SOC 2-aligned infrastructure. You own your data and can export it anytime.

How long does implementation take?

A focused implementation can target 4 weeks; larger programmes commonly take 8–12. Contiship migrated in 6 weeks. Depends on data, integrations, and agreed scope.

Why no per-user pricing?

Per-user pricing creates a perverse incentive — companies limit access to save cost, so key people end up on spreadsheets instead of the ERP. Flat pricing means every employee gets a login without the bill doubling.

Will switching disrupt my finance team?

Your CA can stay on Tally while operations move to Onebook. Two-way sync keeps both sides aligned.

Can you migrate data from Tally, Zoho, or SAP?

Yes. We inspect masters, opening balances, transactions, and workflow dependencies, then document and test the migration before go-live.

What if it doesn't work out for us?

You're not locked in. Onebook includes a lifetime warranty, and your data remains portable. Scope is locked in writing before implementation.
